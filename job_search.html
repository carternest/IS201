My job search plan

  1. Get ready
    • Update your resume and LinkedIn profile
    • List target job titles and must-have skills
    • Decide on location and remote preferences
  2. Find openings
    • Search job boards and company career pages
    • Set up saved searches and email alerts
    • Check the job map below for hot spots
  3. Apply
    • Tailor your resume to each posting
    • Write a short, specific cover note
    • Track every application in a spreadsheet
  4. Interview and follow up
    • Practice with mock interviews
    • Prepare questions for the employer
    • Send a thank-you within 24 hours

Quick tips

5–10 focused applications a week beat 50 rushed ones.

24 hrs is the sweet spot for a thank-you note.

1 tracking spreadsheet keeps it all organized.
